# Project Overview

Build a premium, responsive restaurant website for **Central Perk Chennai**, a Friends-inspired American café and restaurant located in Chennai.

The website should combine:

* Vintage New York café aesthetics
* Cozy coffeehouse atmosphere
* Premium food photography
* Warm lighting
* Brick wall textures
* Neon signage
* Modern restaurant user experience
* Smooth animations and interactions

The website should feel immersive, nostalgic, Instagram-worthy, and professionally designed while maintaining excellent usability across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
